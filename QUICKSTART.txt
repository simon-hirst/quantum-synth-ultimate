QUICKSTART

Do NOT double-click index.html. Modern browsers block module scripts from file:// URLs.

Windows (PowerShell or CMD)
1) cd to the folder
2) start-local.bat
3) publish-tunnel.bat  (optional public URL)

macOS / Linux
1) cd to the folder
2) chmod +x start-local.sh publish-tunnel.sh
3) ./start-local.sh
4) ./publish-tunnel.sh  (optional public URL)

Local URL:  http://localhost:5173
Public URL: printed by cloudflared (https://*.trycloudflare.com)

Audio capture
- Click Share Audio and select a tab/window with Share system audio ticked (Chrome/Edge).
- Mobile browsers usually won't expose system audio capture; test on desktop.
